Edit this photo of a furnished room so that it shows the same room completely empty, as it was before anyone moved in.

Remove everything that is not part of the room itself: all furniture (sofas, footstools, armchairs, chairs, tables, shelving units, cabinets), rugs, lamps, plants, cushions, books, boxes, vases, candle holders, picture frames, posters and any other decoration on the floor, on the furniture or on the walls.

Keep everything that belongs to the room exactly as it is: the same camera position, angle, lens and framing; the same walls and their color, floor, ceiling, skirting boards, windows and their frames, door, radiator and outlets; and the curtains with their rods, as they are. Where something is removed, continue the floor, the walls and the skirting boards naturally, with the same light and shadows of the room. Do not add anything.

The result must look like a real photograph of this same room, empty.
